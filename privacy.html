Privacy Policy

Money Mentor · Effective October 20, 2026

Money Mentor is a personal budgeting app. It is built so that your financial information stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what that means in practice.

The short version

  • We don't collect, store or receive any of your data.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app doesn't connect to any server run by us or by third parties.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter — transactions, accounts, balances, budgets, categories, recurring payments and settings — is saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone. It is not uploaded anywhere by the app. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this data is included in those backups like any other app's data, under Apple's terms and your own settings.

Camera and photos

If you scan a receipt, the camera is used only while the scanner is open. If you pick a receipt from your photo library, the app receives only the image you choose. Text is read from the receipt on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks, and the receipt image is saved with the entry on your iPhone.

AI features

Optional features such as AI insights and receipt reading use Apple Intelligence models that run on your device. Your data is not sent to us or to any AI service.

Widgets

Home Screen widgets show a summary of your balances and budgets. The app shares this summary with its widgets through storage on your device only.

Import and export

When you export your data, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send or save it. Files you import are read on your device.

Deleting your data

You can remove your records at any time with Settings → Erase all data. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
