Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Settings → Export data on the old phone and Settings → Import data on the new one.

How do I lock the app with Face ID?

Touch and hold the Money Mentor icon on the Home Screen and choose Require Face ID.

Why are AI insights unavailable?

They need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in the Settings app.
